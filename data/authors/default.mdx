G'day! I'm Jernej Kavka (JK), a Microsoft AI MVP based in Brisbane, Australia.

I work across .NET, EF Core, Azure, AI, and agentic engineering, with a focus on performance and developer productivity. By day I work as a Solution Architect at [SSW Consulting](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/jernej-kavka/).

My interest in intelligent and AI-adjacent systems goes back to natural-user-interface work: Microsoft Surface table, Kinect, gesture engines, conversational AI, computer vision, and later Cognitive Services. From there I moved through ML.NET and training-data work, and I was using GPT-3 for an [email classification pet project](/speaking/) months before ChatGPT was released — which became my talk at [NDC Sydney 2022](https://ndcsydney.com).
